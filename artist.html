The artist

Tibielle

Owner & nail and lash artist

Available Tue – Sat

Tibielle is the heart and soul of BY TIBIELLE. With over 6 years of experience in nail artistry and lash services, she has built a reputation across Abuja for her incredible attention to detail, her warm personality, and her ability to make every client feel truly pampered.

She trained in professional nail techniques including gel, acrylics, BIAB, and advanced nail art — from minimalist florals to bold 3D designs. On the lash side, she is certified in classic, hybrid, and volume extensions, as well as lash lifts and tints. She founded BY TIBIELLE with a clear mission: to bring international-level beauty services to Abuja, with a boutique feel and without the big-salon chaos.

“I want every client to leave feeling better than when they walked in — not just because of their nails, but because of the whole experience.”
